## Grants & Bounties Pool

**Requested: $5,000 USDC + 15,000 ARROW**

This pool will fund grants and bounties for high-impact Arrow work that isn't covered by other project budgets. Intended areas include:

- **Tokenomics development and documentation**
- **HearHear bot** — update and document the Discord call transcription bot
- **Promotional efforts** — podcast, social media, community growth, etc.
- **Other high-impact work** as identified by the committee

### How the Pool Works

- The GBC will approve spending from the pool by majority committee vote
- Each grant or bounty funded from the pool will be documented in the [dao-gbc repo](https://github.com/Arrow-air/dao-gbc) with scope, amount, recipient, and status
- Unspent funds remain in the GBC multisig and carry forward to the next period
- The GBC will report the remaining balance in future funding requests so the DAO can adjust accordingly

## Accountability & Transparency

All GBC activity will be tracked publicly in the [dao-gbc repo](https://github.com/Arrow-air/dao-gbc):

- **Proposals** — funding requests to the DAO (like this one)
- **Grants** — approved grants with milestones, payments, and status updates
- **Bounties** — open bounties with requirements and claims
- **Reports** — periodic transparency reports on spending and pool balance

## Outcome if Vote Passes

Approval of this proposal will trigger the transfer of USDC and ARROW funds from the Arrow treasury to the [GBC Multisig](https://app.safe.global/home?safe=eth:0x7cFd3D29fD7b13CA33E49bA6b11b79bEF89e5906).

## Outcome if Vote Fails

The GBC will not receive funding for March/April 2026. Grant streams and grants/bounties activity will be paused until a revised proposal is approved.
